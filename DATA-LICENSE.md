## Third party material

The project does not relicense third party records, metadata, abstracts, full
text, API responses, or other source material. Their original terms, copyright
notices, and record-level licences continue to apply. A record being accessible
through MétaCan does not imply that its abstract or source text is covered by
the project CC BY 4.0 licence.

The project uses or documents data from these sources:

- [OpenAlex](https://help.openalex.org/hc/en-us/articles/24396686889751-About-us), whose dataset is released under CC0.
- [NCBI, PubMed, and PubMed Central](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/), subject to NCBI policies and [PubMed Central copyright notices](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/about/copyright/).
- [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/help), where reuse remains subject to the respective rights holder and licence terms.
- [Crossref](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/schema-library/markup-guide-metadata-segments/license-information/), including record-level licence metadata when supplied.
- [Érudit](https://apropos.erudit.org/licenses-et-politiques/?lang=en), whose documents remain subject to journal and rights-holder policies.
- [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/data-api/about-api), through its public data and API documentation.
- [bioRxiv and medRxiv](https://api.biorxiv.org/details/medrxiv/help), whose API exposes a licence field for each record. Author-selected reuse terms govern each preprint.

Project-authored summaries, derived counts, classifier outputs, and
documentation are covered by the project licence only to the extent that they
do not incorporate third party material that remains separately protected.
